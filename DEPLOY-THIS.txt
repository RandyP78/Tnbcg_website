DEPLOY THIS
===========

Drag THIS ZIP FILE onto Netlify. Do not extract it first.

    https://app.netlify.com/projects/tnbcg/deploys

Scroll to "Need to update your project?" and drop the .zip on it.
Netlify unpacks it itself, which is why there is nothing to select and
nothing to get wrong.

AFTER DEPLOYING, THE SITE BEHAVES EXACTLY AS BEFORE
---------------------------------------------------
The four new functions are not linked from any page. Nothing calls
them. A visitor cannot reach them and will not notice any change.

They are inert on purpose. They need environment variables and an
Entra app registration that do not exist yet. Until then, calling one
directly returns an error - which is correct, not a fault.


WHY .mjs
--------
Your three existing functions are CommonJS. The four new ones are ES
modules. They cannot share a package.json "type" setting, so the new
ones use the .mjs extension. This lets both run side by side without
touching your working code. Do not rename them to .js.


WHEN YOU ARE READY TO TURN THE ENGINE ON
-----------------------------------------
See SETUP-REMEDIATION.md. Short version: register the Entra app, add
the environment variables in Netlify, then a UI needs building. None
of that changes anything in this zip.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Netlify keeps every deploy. Go to Deploys, open an earlier one, click
"Publish deploy". You cannot permanently lose the site this way.
