Accessibility

We want this site to be usable by everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, magnification or voice control.

What we aim for

We target WCAG 2.1 Level AA. In building this site we have:

  • Used semantic HTML with a single main heading per page and a logical heading order
  • Kept text contrast at 4.5:1 or better against its background
  • Made every interactive element reachable and operable by keyboard, with a visible focus outline
  • Given every icon-only link an accessible name
  • Labelled all form fields with real labels rather than placeholder text
  • Respected the prefers-reduced-motion setting, so animation is suppressed for anyone who has asked for that
  • Provided a skip link to bypass the header

Known limitations

We are honest about where we fall short rather than claiming full conformance:

  • The network diagram on the homepage carries a text description, but the detail within it is conveyed visually. The same information appears as text in the services list below it.
  • Some third-party content we link to is outside our control and may not meet the same standard.

Tell us if something doesn’t work

If any part of this site creates a barrier for you, please tell us. Email info@tnbcg.com or call 305-419-9992. Include the page and what happened, and we will respond within five business days and tell you how we intend to fix it.

If you need information from this site in another format, ask and we will provide it.
